About the role

Support health screening, safe-water messaging and referral of urgent cases with branch and deployment teams across six wards.

What you will do

  • Support household screening and community mapping.
  • Share approved health and safe-water messages.
  • Record referrals and submit daily activity returns.

Requirements

Active volunteer status, valid identification, safeguarding training, Kiswahili or local-language ability, and availability from 14–25 September.

Commitment and support

Twelve days in Tana River County. Approved travel, accommodation, supervision and applicable stipend support are provided under the final TOR.
