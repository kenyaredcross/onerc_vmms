{#- The look of the two pages in `vmmsx/www/` — sign in, and set a password. **Written out here rather than pulled from `login.bundle.css`.** Frappe's own login stylesheet lays a single centred card on a plain ground, which is a different page from the one this is; keeping it and overriding it would mean every rule below fighting a rule above it, and a framework upgrade quietly changing which one won. So the framework sheet is dropped and this one is complete — including the four rules in it that are *behaviour* rather than decoration, marked below, because `login.js` depends on them and would otherwise show every panel of the page at once. The palette and the type are the portal's, from `portal/tailwind.config.js`, written as literals here for the one reason a literal is ever right in this app: a Jinja page cannot import a Tailwind config. Nothing else about the society is hardcoded — the mark, the name and every sentence are read at render time by `vmmsx/auth_page.py`. Light only, deliberately. This is the same page in every client, and the navy panel *is* the dark surface. -#}
