# Membership Management

The **Membership Management** streamlines the entire lifecycle of membership — from application and payment to approval, certification, and renewal — all integrated within ERPNext and the Volunteer & Member Management System (VMMS).

---

## 1. Registration & Application

Users access the **Member Portal** to apply for membership.

### Steps

1. Log in or create an account.
2. Navigate to the **Membership Page**.
3. Select a **Plan** and **Branch/County** (mapped to ERPNext _Company_).
4. Submit the **Membership Application**, ready for payment.

---

## 2. Payment & Verification

Members can pay directly through integrated payment gateways (MPESA, card, etc.) or via **manual reconciliation** handled by admins.

After payment, the application moves to **admin review**.

---

## 3. Approval & Activation

Admins verify payment and details before approval.

Upon approval:

- A **Membership Record** is activated.
- A **Certificate** is auto-generated.

---

## 4. Certificates

Each approved membership includes a **downloadable digital certificate** with:

- Member name, plan, branch, and validity period
- Auto-generation on approval
- PDF format with branding support

Accessible via the **Membership** section of the portal.

---

## 5. Multi-Branch Membership

Members can join several branches without duplicating profiles.
Each membership links to a single **Company (Branch)**, while reports remain centralized for national oversight.

---

## 6. Renewals

Renewals occur directly through the portal:

1. Member views upcoming or expired memberships.
2. Initiates renewal and completes payment.
3. System extends validity automatically.
